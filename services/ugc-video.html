UGC Video

Authentic, creator-style video content scripted, shot and edited to feel native on the feed — built to perform as ads, not just look like content.

What's included

  • UGC ad videos
  • Testimonial-style videos
  • Product demo videos
  • Reels / TikTok-ready content
  • Scripting & creator briefing
  • Editing & captioning

Why it matters

Polished, studio-style ads increasingly get scrolled past. UGC-style video — shot to look like it came from a real person, not a brand — earns attention because it doesn't look like an ad, even when it's built entirely for performance.

Every video is scripted around a hook, a clear product moment and a call to action, then edited for the platform it's running on — so it's ready to plug straight into your Meta, TikTok or Instagram campaigns.
