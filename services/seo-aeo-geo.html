What AEO and GEO mean

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) focuses on structuring content so it can be surfaced directly as an answer — in featured snippets, voice search and AI assistants.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) focuses on how your brand and content are represented and cited within generative AI search experiences and chat-based answers.

Both work alongside traditional SEO — they don't replace it, they extend your visibility to where search behavior is heading. SEO, AEO and GEO are optimization and visibility strategies, not a guarantee of specific rankings or AI Overview placement.
